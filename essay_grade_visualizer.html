Essay Writing Rules Cheat Sheet

Capitalization Rules Always capitalize the first letter of sentences, the pronoun "I", and proper nouns (names, specific places, days, months).
Punctuation Essentials Don't create run-on sentences! Use periods to separate full thoughts. Use commas after introductory phrases and before conjunctions (and, but, so).
Paragraph Structure (PEEL) Point: State your idea. Evidence: Give proof. Explanation: Explain why it matters. Link: Transition to next point.
